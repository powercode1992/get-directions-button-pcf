## Third-party maps & routing

This control does not generate routes itself. It opens a deep link to an external
maps provider selected via the `gc_Provider` property:

- **Google Maps** — https://www.google.com/maps
- **Microsoft Bing Maps** — https://www.bing.com/maps
- **Apple Maps** — https://maps.apple.com
- **OpenStreetMap** — https://www.openstreetmap.org

Routing, distance, geocoding and map data are supplied by, and remain the property
of, those providers and their data contributors. Their accuracy, availability and
terms of use are the sole responsibility of each provider. **Verify all routes
before travel. Use at your own risk.**
